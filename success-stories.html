FEATURED SCHOLAR CHRONICLE

The Journey of Priyansh: From Grade 6 Hesitation to PRMO Medalist

When Priyansh joined Pragyan in Grade 6, he found multi-step arithmetic word problems intimidating. Standard school classes rewarded speed over understanding, leaving him second-guessing his steps.

Under the guidance of our mathematics faculty, Priyansh spent six months learning to visualize geometry with ruler-and-compass sketches and decomposing integers through prime factorization trees.

MONTH 1–3: AXIOMATIC REBUILD

Eliminated Formula Memorization

Mastered first-principles arithmetic and basic Euclidean angle theorems.

MONTH 6: FIRST PODIUM

Zonal Silver Medal in IMO Class 6

Achieved 98.4 percentile in Level 1 and secured State Rank 4 in Level 2.

GRADE 9: NATIONAL RECOGNITION

PRMO Qualified with 46/60 Marks

Ranked among the top 1% junior mathematicians nationwide.
