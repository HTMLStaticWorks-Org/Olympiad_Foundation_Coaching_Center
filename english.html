PRECISION COMMUNICATION

Beyond Conversational Fluency: Academic Mastery

The International English Olympiad (IEO) tests nuanced grammar mechanics, contextual word nuance, idioms, rhetorical structures, and the ability to interpret multi-layered narrative prose under tight time constraints.

Our program approaches the English language with the same analytical rigor as mathematics. Students study word roots (Greek and Latin etymology), syntax trees, phrasal collocations, and formal logical rhetoric.

Etymological Roots

Decoding prefixes, roots, and suffixes to unlock the meaning of unfamiliar vocabulary instantly.

Syntactical Grammar

Subject-verb agreement, subjunctive mood, dangling modifiers, and subtle tense shifts.

Speed Comprehension

Skimming for thesis statements, identifying author tone, and extracting implicit evidence.

Verbal Reasoning

Sentence completion, paragraph jumbles, logical coherence, and contextual register.
