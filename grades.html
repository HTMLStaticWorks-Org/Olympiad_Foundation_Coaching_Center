STRUCTURED DEVELOPMENTAL ROADMAPS

Grade-Wise Learning Pathways (Grades 1 to 10)

Tailored pedagogical stages designed to match cognitive readiness, from playful concrete patterns in early primary years to rigorous mathematical proofs in secondary grades.

DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE 01

Foundation Stage: Grades 1 – 3

Cognitive Focus: Intuitive number sense, concrete geometric manipulation, observational science, and playful non-verbal patterns.

At this stage, we deliberately avoid pressurized exam environments. Classes are highly interactive and tactile, using tangible blocks, balance scales, and story-based puzzles to establish an instinctive love for discovery.

Subjects Covered:

Math Intuition, Science Curiosities, Pattern Play, Early English

Target Competitions:

IMO Level 1, NSO Level 1, Primary Kangourou

DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE 02

Development Stage: Grades 4 – 6

Cognitive Focus: Transitioning from concrete arithmetic to formal algebraic notation, systematic geometry, and physical science laws.

Students learn to organize multi-step calculations, decompose complex word problems into clear variable expressions, and understand the scientific method behind physical experiments.

Subjects Covered:

Algebra Basics, Euclidean Angles, Kinetic Physics, IEO Verbal

Target Competitions:

NMTC Primary, IMO Level 2, NSO, SpellBee

Enquire for Grades 4–6 →
DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE 03

Advanced Foundation: Grades 7 – 8

Cognitive Focus: Rigorous mathematical proof construction, chemical equation dynamics, Newtonian mechanics, and formal deductive syllogisms.

This is the crucial bridge to high-school competitive excellence. Students tackle past papers from PRMO, NMTC Sub-Junior, and NSEJS Stage 1 under timed conditions with comprehensive error log reviews.

Subjects Covered:

Number Theory, Congruence, Stoichiometry, Optics, PRMO Prep

Target Competitions:

PRMO, NMTC Sub-Junior, NSEJS, NSO Stage 2

Enquire for Grades 7–8 →
Advanced Proofs & Diagnostics Grades 7 – 8 Cohort
National Competitive Sprints Grades 9 – 10 Cohort
DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE 04

Competitive Preparation: Grades 9 – 10

Cognitive Focus: National Olympiad Stage 2 qualifying standards, RMO/INMO mathematical proofs, IJSO national camp preparation, and early foundation for JEE/NEET.

Students learn advanced topics like calculus fundamentals, combinatorics, organic reaction mechanisms, and kinematics under the guidance of former Olympiad medalists.

Subjects Covered:

RMO Level Math, IJSO Physics & Chem, Advanced NTSE Aptitude

Target Competitions:

RMO, INMO, IJSO, NSEJS, NTSE National
